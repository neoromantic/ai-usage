Privacy change: your projects now reach only the teammates who work in the same repository. Until now each machine sent its 12 largest project folders to the relay, their paths sealed with the team key, so any machine of the team could read them, though no report showed them. From this release a project goes to the team only when its repository has an `origin`, without its path, and sealed with a key made of the team key and the repository's address: a machine that works in the same repository reads it, and the others cannot.

## Shared projects

When another machine of the team works in one of your repositories, PROJECTS shows what it spent there, in a new TEAM column, and that machine's detail in the interactive view lists SHARED PROJECTS: your projects it works in too, with its tokens in each. The menu bar app's Projects tab shows the team's tokens beside a shared project, and its details name each machine with its share. A project only you work in stays on your machine: no other machine sees its name or its tokens.

- One repository is one `origin`, however it was cloned: `git@github.com:acme/api.git` and `https://github.com/acme/api` match, and worktrees count with their repository. A fork is a repository of its own, and so is an address with an SSH host alias, such as `github-work:acme/api`.
- A project with no `origin`, a folder outside any repository, and on macOS a repository in Documents, Desktop, or another folder the system asks about before an app reads it, go to nobody.
- Two machines of one person share projects the way two people's do.
- Each account sends up to 128 projects, up from 12, while the snapshot stays within 64 KB: one account's 128 take about 28 KB.

A teammate who has the team key and guesses the address of one of your repositories, a public one for example, can read your use of it. The relay's operator cannot read any of them.

## For a team

- **Relay:** update your relay before your machines install this release. A relay before v0.14.0 refuses more than 12 projects an account; the machines then send their 12 largest and try again a day later, so nothing breaks, but the team shares fewer projects until the relay is updated.
- **Older releases:** while any machine of the team runs a release before v0.14.0, the others send at most 12 projects an account, as such a release drops a snapshot with more. A machine on an older release shows no shared projects, and goes on sending its 12 largest project paths sealed with the team key, as before, until it updates. Snapshots it sent before stay on the relay until its next run replaces them.
