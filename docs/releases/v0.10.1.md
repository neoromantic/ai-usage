## The menu bar app follows a brew upgrade you run yourself

After `brew upgrade neoromantic/tap/ai-usage`, `ai-usage update` and the menu bar app's Check for Updates said "up to date" and left the app at the earlier release, until a scheduled run brought it along. Now they bring the app to the release Homebrew installed, and say `installed the menu bar app of v0.10.1` when they do.

Copies the installer put in place were not affected: they keep the app at their release at every check.

## Upgrade

Homebrew installs update with `brew upgrade neoromantic/tap/ai-usage` or `ai-usage update`; script installs update at a scheduled run.

## For the website

Nothing the website shows changed: install commands, the report, the JSON, and the demo pictures are as in v0.10.0.
